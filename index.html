Common questions

A few helpful answers.

Do you provide pest treatments?

No. This is an independent educational and referral website. We do not inspect properties, provide quotes, or perform treatments.

Does a San Gabriel Valley address guarantee coverage?

No. Referral eligibility may depend on your exact ZIP code, pest, property type, and the provider. Our focus is the San Gabriel Valley communities listed on this site. Network ZIP eligibility and provider availability are still pending.

Can I identify a pest from a single sign?

Often you need more evidence. Photograph what you see, record where and when it appeared, and ask a qualified professional for identification before choosing treatment.

Where does the guidance come from?

Start with UC IPM household pest resources and University of California IPM principles. Our guides summarize general prevention ideas and do not replace a property-specific assessment.
