Survival Pest Control is an independent pest information and referral website created for survivalpestcontrol.com. We publish basic identification and prevention resources for homeowners, renters, and property managers.

Our area

We focus on the San Gabriel Valley in California, including Temple City, Pasadena, Alhambra, El Monte, West Covina, Arcadia, Monrovia, and Baldwin Park. The 626 area is a geographic reference; referral eligibility must be checked by property ZIP code.

Referral status

This site may receive compensation for qualifying referral connections. Providers will be responsible for inspections, advice, quotes, scheduling, and work. Confirm eligibility and service details directly with the provider.
