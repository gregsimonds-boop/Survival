Cockroaches: signs and sensible next steps.

Know what is hiding around food and moisture.

What to look for

Note the size, color, location, and time of sightings. Repeated sightings or small roaches in kitchens warrant closer attention.

What you can do today

Clean grease and crumbs, remove clutter, keep food sealed, and fix plumbing leaks. Inspect boxes before bringing them indoors.

When to ask a professional

Repeated sightings, young roaches, or activity in multiple rooms are reasons to seek professional assessment. In shared buildings, notify management.

Before you call

Write down when the issue began, where you see signs, and any steps already taken. Have photos and your ZIP code ready. Ask whether the provider handles this pest and property type, and request clear pricing and follow-up terms.
